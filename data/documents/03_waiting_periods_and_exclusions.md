# Nunez Secure Insurance – SecureCare Plus Waiting Periods and Exclusions

> **Synthetic training document.** This fictional wording is created only for the RAGAS Evaluation Lab.

## 1. Initial waiting period
A **30-day initial waiting period** applies from the first policy commencement date for illnesses. This initial waiting period does not apply to claims arising solely from an accident, subject to the policy terms.

## 2. Pre-existing diseases
A **36-month continuous coverage waiting period** applies to pre-existing diseases, counted from the first policy commencement date, subject to applicable portability and continuity credits recorded by the insurer.

## 3. Specified procedures
A **24-month waiting period** applies to the specified procedures listed in the policy schedule. The exact list of specified procedures is in the individual policy schedule; this training knowledge base does not reproduce that list.

## 4. Exclusions
The following are excluded under the sample policy:
- treatment taken solely for cosmetic or aesthetic purposes, unless medically necessary following an accident or cancer treatment as specified by the policy;
- routine dental treatment, unless required due to an accident and otherwise admissible under the policy;
- expenses for non-medical items that are not listed as payable in the policy schedule.

## 5. Coverage assessment
A treatment may be payable only when it meets the policy's medical-necessity requirements, applicable waiting periods have been completed, it is not excluded, and all other policy conditions are met. If the policy documents supplied here do not address a treatment or service, its coverage must not be assumed.
