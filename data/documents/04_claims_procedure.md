# Nunez Secure Insurance – SecureCare Plus Claims Procedure

> **Synthetic training document.** This fictional claims process is created only for the RAGAS Evaluation Lab.

## 1. Cashless claim
Cashless treatment is available only at a hospital in the insurer's network, and is subject to pre-authorization and approval by the insurer or its appointed Third-Party Administrator (TPA).

Typical process:
1. The insured person visits a network hospital.
2. The hospital submits a pre-authorization request with relevant medical information.
3. The insurer or TPA reviews the request against policy terms, waiting periods, exclusions and available sum insured.
4. If approved, eligible expenses are settled directly with the network hospital, subject to final bill review and policy limits.
5. The insured person pays non-payable items and any amount not approved under the policy.

A pre-authorization approval is not a guarantee that every item in the final bill will be payable.

## 2. Emergency admission notification
For an emergency hospitalization, the policyholder or a representative must notify the insurer within **48 hours of admission**.

## 3. Reimbursement claim
If treatment is taken at a non-network hospital, or cashless treatment is not used, the policyholder may submit a reimbursement claim, subject to policy terms.

The claim submission should include:
- completed claim form;
- discharge summary;
- original hospital bills and payment receipts;
- prescriptions and investigation reports; and
- any additional documents reasonably requested for claim assessment.

## 4. Claim acknowledgement and decision
The insurer aims to acknowledge receipt of a complete claim submission within **2 working days**. It aims to communicate a claim decision within **10 working days after receiving all required documents**. These are service targets and may be affected by the need for additional information or investigation.

## 5. Reimbursement payment
For an approved reimbursement claim, payment is targeted within **5 working days after approval**, subject to successful verification of bank details and other payment requirements.
