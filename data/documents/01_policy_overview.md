# Nunez Secure Insurance – SecureCare Plus Policy Overview

> **Synthetic training document.** Nunez Secure Insurance and SecureCare Plus are fictional. This document is created only for the RAGAS Evaluation Lab and does not describe a real insurance product.

## 1. Product summary
SecureCare Plus is an individual and family-floater health insurance policy intended to help pay eligible medical expenses for covered illness or injury during the policy period, subject to policy terms, limits, waiting periods and exclusions.

## 2. Policy period and sum insured
The policy is issued for a period of one year and may be renewed subject to the policy terms. The standard sample plan used in this evaluation dataset has a sum insured of **₹5,00,000 per policy year**. The sum insured is the maximum overall amount available for eligible claims in a policy year, subject to applicable sub-limits and policy conditions.

## 3. Covered hospitalization
Eligible inpatient hospitalization expenses are covered when:
- the admission is medically necessary;
- the insured person is admitted for at least 24 consecutive hours; and
- the treatment is not excluded and applicable waiting periods have been completed.

The 24-hour minimum does not apply to eligible day-care procedures listed in the policy schedule. Such procedures may be covered even when hospital admission is shorter than 24 hours, subject to medical necessity, the policy schedule and exclusions.

## 4. Policyholder responsibilities
The policyholder must provide accurate information in the proposal and claim documents, pay premiums by the due date, and notify the insurer of a claim within the timelines stated in the claims procedure.

## 5. Important note
Coverage is never determined by the name of a treatment alone. The diagnosis, medical necessity, provider, policy schedule, waiting periods, exclusions and available sum insured may all affect claim eligibility.
