# Nunez Secure Insurance – SecureCare Plus Benefits and Limits

> **Synthetic training document.** This fictional benefit schedule is created only for the RAGAS Evaluation Lab.

## 1. Base sum insured
The sample SecureCare Plus plan has a base sum insured of **₹5,00,000 per policy year**.

## 2. Room rent limit
Eligible standard room rent is limited to **1% of the base sum insured per day**. For the sample ₹5,00,000 plan, the room rent limit is **₹5,000 per day**.

## 3. Intensive Care Unit (ICU) limit
Eligible ICU room charges are limited to **2% of the base sum insured per day**. For the sample ₹5,00,000 plan, the ICU limit is **₹10,000 per day**.

## 4. Pre- and post-hospitalization expenses
Eligible medical expenses related to a covered hospitalization are covered for:
- **30 days before** the date of admission; and
- **60 days after** the date of discharge.

These expenses must be medically related to the covered hospitalization, supported by bills and prescriptions, and remain subject to the overall sum insured and policy terms.

## 5. Limits and claim assessment
The room rent and ICU limits are sub-limits, not additional amounts over and above the sum insured. Payment is also subject to medical necessity, admissibility of expenses, exclusions, waiting periods and the remaining sum insured.
